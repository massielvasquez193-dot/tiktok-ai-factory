# First Customer Guide

> Welcome to TikTok AI Factory! This guide walks you through setting up your account and generating your first AI video in under 10 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account (2 min)

1. Visit [ttvideoai.com/register](https://ttvideoai.com/register)
2. Enter your name, email, and password
3. Click "Create account"
4. Check your email for the verification link (or check server logs in mock mode)

## Step 2: Create Your Workspace (1 min)

1. After login, you&apos;ll be prompted to create a workspace
2. Name it after your brand or business (e.g., "My Beauty Brand")
3. Your workspace is created with a Free plan — 50 credits included

## Step 3: Add Your First Product (2 min)

1. Go to **Products** in the sidebar
2. Click "Add Product"
3. Enter your product name, category, and upload images
4. Click "Save"

## Step 4: Generate Your First Script (2 min)

1. Go to **Scripts** in the sidebar
2. Select your product
3. Choose script type: **UGC** (user-generated content) is great for TikTok
4. Choose language: English, Malay, Thai, Filipino, Spanish, or Chinese
5. Click "Generate Script" (costs 5 credits)
6. Review and edit the AI-generated script

## Step 5: Generate Your First Video (3 min)

1. After approving your script, click "Generate Storyboard"
2. Review the scene breakdown
3. Click "Generate Video"
4. Your video enters the queue — status updates in real-time
5. When complete, find it in **Video Library**

## Step 6: Publish (Optional)

1. Go to **Publishing Center**
2. Select your video
3. Choose platform: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or Instagram Reels
4. Add title, description, and hashtags
5. Click "Publish Now" or schedule for later

---

## What&apos;s Next?

- **Upgrade your plan**: Go to Settings → Billing to unlock more credits
- **Invite your team**: Go to Settings → Members to add collaborators
- **Browse templates**: Visit the Template Marketplace for pre-built pipelines
- **Configure AI providers**: Go to Settings → Providers to use your own API keys
- **Explore automation**: Set up automated workflows in the Workflow Builder
